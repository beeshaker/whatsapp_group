How it works

No migration, no training, no new app. Whats2Manage works quietly inside the WhatsApp groups you already use.

1

Post in the group, like always

Tenants and staff keep using the same WhatsApp group they already use.

2

AI reads and classifies it

New issues are detected, sorted by category (plumbing, electrical, security, and more) and priority — one message can even split into multiple tickets.

3

Nothing falls through the cracks

Deadlines, auto-escalation, and reminders keep tickets moving. A 👍 reaction in the group marks it as being handled.

4

You always know what's going on

A dashboard for your whole team, daily WhatsApp summaries, and a chat assistant you can ask "what's still open?" anytime.
